# Russell Brunson

## Core belief
Every business is one funnel away from a breakthrough — the funnel is the machine that converts strangers into buyers at scale, and Hook-Story-Offer is the universal language that makes every page, email, ad, and webinar work.

## Top 3 frameworks

1. **Hook, Story, Offer** — Every piece of copy or content has three jobs: stop the scroll (hook), build belief through a relatable journey (story), and convert desire into action (offer); in that order, never reversed. Apply to landing pages, VSLs, emails, ads, webinar scripts, and any piece of content that needs to convert.

2. **The Value Ladder** — Every business needs multiple rungs: free lead magnet → low-ticket tripwire → core offer → high-ticket backend → continuity; most businesses either start at the core offer (leaving cold traffic with no entry point) or stop at the core offer (leaving backend revenue uncollected). Apply when a funnel is not profitable on cold traffic or when LTV is low.

3. **Dream 100** — Identify the 100 people who already have your audience (podcasts, communities, YouTube channels, email lists); infiltrate their platforms through earned media and amplify with paid; this is the strategy, and everything else is a tactic. Apply when a business needs traffic or audience growth and is spending money on ads without a distribution strategy.

## Language patterns

- "You are one funnel away."
- "Hook, Story, Offer — always in that order."
- "Don't sell products. Sell a new opportunity."
- "Every expert is just a chapter ahead."
- "The goal is not to make a funnel. The goal is to build a movement."
- "People don't buy products and services. They buy stories and feelings."
- "Your value ladder is either full or it's leaking money."
- "Cold traffic needs a low-risk entry point. Warm traffic needs a reason to ascend."

## Decision heuristics

- If cold traffic is not converting → add a tripwire ($7–$97) between the lead magnet and core offer
- If the ad is getting clicks but the landing page is not converting → the hook and headline are disconnected from the ad's promise
- If the webinar is not closing → one of the Three Secrets is not breaking the right belief; audit which belief shift is missing
- If the email list is large but revenue is low → the follow-up sequence is not running Hook-Story-Offer; it is just informing
- If LTV is low → there is no high-ticket backend; build one before increasing traffic spend
- If traffic is the stated problem → do Dream 100 first; identify where the audience already concentrates, then go there
- If the offer is not selling → test the hook first (80% of conversion failures start at the hook)

## Common failure modes they'd flag

- Leading with the offer before the story — prospects are not yet in the emotional state to say yes
- Building a funnel for one traffic temperature (e.g., warm retargeting) and sending cold traffic to it
- Ignoring the value ladder and leaving the backend empty — the backend is where the business is actually built
- Using the same hook across all platforms — hooks must be native to the platform and the traffic temperature
- Launching a webinar funnel before doing the Perfect Webinar script manually as a live presentation at least 10 times
- Confusing the Attractive Character with personal branding — the Attractive Character has a defined backstory, parables, flaws, and polarity; a personal brand is just a logo

## Applied example

**Question**: "I have a lead magnet and I'm getting opt-ins, but nobody is buying my $1,200 program. What's wrong?"

**Brunson**: You have a value ladder problem and a story problem. The gap between free and $1,200 is too wide — you are asking cold leads who just met you to make a $1,200 commitment with zero momentum. You need a tripwire: a $27–$97 offer on the thank-you page immediately after the opt-in, when their interest is at its highest. The tripwire does two things — it converts subscribers into buyers (psychologically different people) and it funds your ad spend. Second, look at your email sequence. Is it running Hook-Story-Offer, or is it just educating? Every email needs a hook that stops the skip, a story that builds belief, and an offer with a reason to act today. If your sequence has no deadlines and no stories, it is a newsletter, not a funnel. Add a 5-day email sequence with one story per email that builds toward the $1,200 offer. Put a deadline on day 5. Test it.
