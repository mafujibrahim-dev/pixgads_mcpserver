### 1. Pillar Content + Cascade (Vaynerchuk)

**Pillar content** (1 per week):
One long-form piece that becomes the source of truth for the week's distribution. Options:
- 30–60 min podcast / YouTube interview or solo episode
- 2,000–4,000 word LinkedIn article or newsletter
- Live workshop or Q&A (repurposable)
- Behind-the-scenes process documentation

**Cascade** (atomise 1 pillar → 30+ pieces):

| Piece | Platform | Format |
|-------|---------|--------|
| 3–5 short clips | TikTok / Reels / Shorts | 30–90 sec vertical video |
| 5–7 standalone quotes | Twitter/X | Text posts with context |
| 2–3 carousel breakdowns | LinkedIn / Instagram | 7–10 slide carousel |
| 1 email | Owned list | Summary + CTA |
| 1 blog post | SEO | Expanded written version |
| 1–2 audiograms | Podcast / Shorts | Key audio moments with captions |
| Community post | Facebook / Discord / Slack | Discussion prompt from the episode |

**Cascade rule** (Vaynerchuk): Reformatting is not optional — platform-native or it fails. A talking-head clip that works on YouTube needs captions, jump cuts, and a hook sub-3-seconds for TikTok.

### 2. Platform-Native Adaptations

Prioritise platforms based on where the user's buyer already concentrates attention. Pick 2–3 to own before spreading thin.

| Platform | What works | Posting frequency |
|---------|-----------|-----------------|
| LinkedIn | Long-form text, carousel, 1-minute video | 3–5x/week |
| TikTok / Reels | Fast hook, native feel, trending audio | 1–2x/day |
| YouTube | 8–20 min tutorial or story, SEO titles | 1–2x/week |
| Twitter/X | Hot takes, threads, reply volume | 3–5x/day |
| Email | Value-first, single CTA, personal tone | 2–3x/week |
| Podcast | Deep dive, interviews, 20–45 min | 1x/week |

### 3. Brunson Hook Stack — 10 Scroll-Stoppers

For every piece of pillar content, generate 10 hook variations to test across formats. Each hook is a pattern interrupt that makes the audience stop.

**Hook categories**:
1. **Curiosity gap**: "Most [niche] don't know this, and it's costing them [X]…"
2. **Bold claim**: "[Specific counterintuitive statement] — here's why."
3. **Pain/problem**: "If you've been [struggling with X], this is why."
4. **Story open**: "The day I [pivotal moment] changed everything."
5. **Number**: "[Specific number] things [niche] does wrong that kills [result]."
6. **Proof**: "I went from [before] to [after] in [timeframe]. Here's the exact process."
7. **Contrarian**: "Stop [commonly recommended advice]. It doesn't work because…"
8. **Direct address**: "[Specific audience member] — this is for you."
9. **Question**: "Why does [thing] work for some [niche] and not others?"
10. **Warning**: "Before you [common action], read this."

**Brunson rule**: The hook is the offer for the rest of the content. If the hook does not promise something specific the audience wants, they scroll.

### 4. Jab / Jab / Right Hook Ratio (Vaynerchuk)

| Content type | Ratio | Examples |
|-------------|-------|---------|
| Jab (give) | 3–5 out of every 6 pieces | Tips, tutorials, behind-the-scenes, stories, opinions |
| Right hook (ask) | 1 out of every 6 pieces | Buy now, book a call, join the list, download this |

**Jab content that primes buyers**:
- Shows results (social proof without selling)
- Demonstrates the method (builds belief)
- Documents the process (builds parasocial trust)
- Shares a client win (triggers aspiration)

**Right hook rules**:
- One ask per right hook — never stack CTAs
- The ask must be the obvious next step from the jabs that preceded it
- Urgency and specificity increase response: "For the next 48 hours…" beats "click here"

### 5. Distribution Cadence + Attribution Plan

**Week 1–4 (Foundation)**:
- Choose 2 primary platforms and post every day
- Document performance: saves, shares, comments, DMs, link clicks
- Identify top 3 performing hooks/topics

**Week 5–8 (Amplification)**:
- Boost top organic performers with paid spend (≥$10/day minimum)
- Double down on winning hook/topic categories
- Add one owned-channel push per week (email or SMS)

**Week 9–12 (Scale)**:
- Introduce Dream 100 strategy (Brunson): identify 10 accounts with your audience, engage authentically daily
- Begin guest appearances / collaborations for cross-distribution
- Set up retargeting: content viewers → offer page → email sequence

**Attribution tracking** (minimum viable):

| Metric | Track how | Frequency |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Organic reach by platform | Native analytics | Weekly |
| Email list growth | ESP dashboard | Weekly |
| Inbound DMs / inquiries | Manual log | Daily |
| Content → conversion path | UTM links on all CTAs | Per campaign |
| Cost per lead (paid) | Ad manager | Daily when live |
