### 3. Guarantee Stack

**Conditional guarantee**: "Complete [specific steps] within [timeframe] and if you do not achieve [specific result], we will [remedy]."

**Unconditional guarantee**: "[X]-day money-back, no questions asked."

Choose based on fulfilment confidence:
- High confidence in results → lead with results-based
- High volume / lower-touch → lead with unconditional

---

### 4. Price Anchoring + Premium Positioning (Kennedy)

- **Anchor statement**: "Most [category] charge $[competitor price] and deliver [inferior result]. This offer delivers [superior result] for $[price]."
- **Positioning line**: [One sentence USP — specific, provable, non-generic]
- **Who it is NOT for**: [Kennedy exclusion statement — repels the wrong buyer, elevates the right one]

---

### 5. Urgency + Scarcity Mechanic (Kennedy deadline funnel)

Choose one genuine mechanic:
- **Date deadline**: "[Offer/bonus] expires [date]. After that, [consequence]."
- **Quantity cap**: "Only [N] spots. [X] are already taken."
- **Cohort model**: "Next intake: [date]. Application closes [earlier date]."
- **Price increase**: "Price goes to $[higher] on [date]."

**Rule**: Never fake the deadline. If it resets, trust is permanently damaged.

---

### 5. Message-to-Market Match Line (Kennedy)

One headline-ready sentence that uses the exact language the buyer uses internally:

> "[Specific result] for [specific person] in [specific timeframe] — guaranteed or [remedy]."

This line should be usable as: email subject line, ad headline, landing page H1, and verbal pitch opener.
