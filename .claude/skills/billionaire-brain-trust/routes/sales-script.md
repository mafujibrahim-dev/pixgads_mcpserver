## Output: Straight-Line Script + 10X Follow-Up Cadence

When this route is active, produce a usable script and cadence customised to the user's product, price point, and buyer. Do not explain the framework in the output — fill it in with their specifics.

---

### 1. Opening: Tonality + 4-Second Rapport (Belfort)

**Objective**: In the first four seconds, establish yourself as:
- Sharp and authoritative (they are talking to someone worth their time)
- Enthusiastic but controlled (certain, not needy)

**Opening formula**:
> "Hey [Name], it's [Your Name] from [Company] — glad I caught you. I'll be quick — [one sentence bridge to why this call matters to them specifically]. I actually have a couple of quick questions if you've got two minutes. Does that work?"

**Tonality**: Downward inflection on your name and company (certainty). Slight upward energy on "glad I caught you" (warmth). Slow down on the bridge sentence — it is the hook.

---

### 2. Needs Discovery Loop (Belfort — Three Tens)

**Objective**: Build all Three Tens — certainty about the product, the company, and you.

**Discovery questions** (customise to product):

*Ten 1 — Product certainty (they need to believe the product works)*
- "What have you tried before to solve [problem]? What happened?"
- "What would solving [problem] mean for you in the next 90 days?"
- "On a scale of 1–10, how important is fixing this right now?"

*Ten 2 — Company certainty (they need to trust the organisation)*
- Weave in: social proof, client results, years in business, guarantee
- "We've helped [N] people in [their situation] achieve [specific result]…"

*Ten 3 — You certainty (they need to trust and like you)*
- Active listening: use their exact words back to them
- Match their pace and energy
- Share a brief, relevant personal anecdote

**Diagnosis before pitching**: Score each Ten 1–10 before presenting the offer. Do not pitch until all Three are ≥7.

---

### 3. Objection Looping (Belfort)

Three objection categories and their loops:

**Money objection**: "It's too expensive / I can't afford it"
1. Agree: "I completely understand — [price] is a real investment."
2. Isolate: "Let me ask — is it the price itself, or is it that you're not sure the return will be there?"
3. Reframe: "If you were certain you'd get [specific result], would the investment make sense?"
4. Close: "So really, what we're deciding is whether [result] is worth [price] to you — and based on what you told me earlier about [their stated pain], it sounds like it is. Can we move forward?"

**Decision objection**: "I need to think about it / talk to my partner"
1. Agree: "Absolutely, I want you to feel confident."
2. Isolate: "Is it the [product / price / timing] you want to think through?"
3. Reframe: "Most of my clients who say that tell me later they wish they'd decided sooner. What I can do is walk you through everything so you have all you need to decide today."
4. Close: "[Ask for the decision again with a specific action]"

**Trust objection**: "I'm not sure this will work for me"
1. Agree: "That's a fair concern — and I wouldn't expect you to just take my word for it."
2. Reframe: Lead with the most relevant case study / testimonial
3. Add guarantee: "And if it doesn't work, here's exactly what we do… [state guarantee]"
4. Close: "Given that, does it feel safe to move forward?"

**Loop limit**: Maximum 3 loops on the same objection. If unresolved after 3, either re-qualify or release the prospect.

### 4. Cardone 12× Follow-Up Sequence

Most deals close between contact 5 and contact 12. Deploy this cadence after an inconclusive call:

| Contact | Day | Channel | Message focus |
|---------|-----|---------|--------------|
| 1 | Day 0 | Call | "Great talking — sending you the details now" |
| 2 | Day 0 | Email | Recap + offer summary + CTA |
| 3 | Day 1 | Text/DM | Short: "Did you get a chance to look it over?" |
| 4 | Day 2 | Call | "Wanted to answer any questions" |
| 5 | Day 4 | Email | Case study / testimonial relevant to their situation |
| 6 | Day 6 | Call | Soft urgency: "A few spots filling up…" |
| 7 | Day 8 | Email | Address the most common objection proactively |
| 8 | Day 11 | Call | Re-qualify: "Is this still a priority for you?" |
| 9 | Day 14 | Text/DM | Value-add: share a quick tip or insight |
| 10 | Day 18 | Email | New case study or result |
| 11 | Day 22 | Call | "I want to make sure I'm not wasting your time…" |
| 12 | Day 30 | Email | Breakup email: "Should I close your file?" |

**Cardone rule**: The follow-up IS the service. Persistence is not harassment — it is commitment to the prospect's result.

---

### 5. Close Language with Takeaway (Belfort + Cardone)

**Standard close**:
> "Based on everything we've talked about, it sounds like [product] is exactly what you need to [their stated goal]. The next step is [specific action]. Can we do that today?"

**Takeaway close** (use when resistance is high):
> "I want to be straight with you — this might not be the right fit. We work best with people who are ready to [committed action]. Is that you right now?"

**Assumptive close**:
> "Let's get you started. I just need [email / card / signature]. What's the best [email / address] for you?"

**The silence rule**: After the closing question, do not speak. The next person who speaks loses the frame. Wait.
